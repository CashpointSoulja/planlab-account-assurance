# Worked account: National Grid Electricity Transmission

Role-play only. No outreach has been sent. 'From Planlab' lines are a practice script for use only after authorisation to represent Planlab, not a claim that Ayo works there today.

## Why this account
Ofgem's9December2025 decision on EGL3,EGL4 and Grimsby–Walpole questioned QSRA assumptions and noted that fastest delivery scenarios and acceleration opportunities were not modelled. This is a public regulatory finding, not evidence of a current software gap or a licence to criticise the team's work. The first discovery test is who owns the scenario analysis and whether repeat runs are actually a constraint. Strategic relevance is high; near-term accessibility is unknown and procurement may be slow.
Source: https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2025-12/material-scope-change-decision.pdf
Product fit: Planlab says its QSRA maps risks to programme activities, runs Monte Carlo and outputsP50/P80. It loads risks fromOPCorExcel. Scenario exploration creates what-if copies, leaving live plan untouched.
Sources: https://www.planlab.ai/qsra ; https://www.planlab.ai/scenario-exploration

## 30-second opening
Hi [name], it's Ayo from Planlab. This is a cold call. Can I have30seconds, then you tell me if it's relevant? I read Ofgem's December decision on the Lincolnshire projects and the point about testing acceleration scenarios. Planlab helps planning teams compare schedule options. Who owns those scenario assumptions and re-runs in your team?

## Discovery
- Which named programme and schedule outcomes do you own?
- How are contractor schedules and risk assumptions brought together, and what tools do you use?
- When an assumption changes, how long does a useful scenario re-run take? Is that actually a problem?
- What's the next dated planning/reporting event where a faster comparison would matter?
- Who reviews outputs, and what security/procurement route would any trial need?

## Objections
Already have tools/consultants: Makes sense. I'm not assuming you need another tool. Is there any part of repeat scenario work that still takes longer than you'd like, or is the current setup working well?
Security: Fair concern. Planlab's public security page listsISO27001andCyberEssentialsPlus, withSOC2TypeIIinprogress. Your security team would need to verify the controls and requirements with the product team. I wouldn't promise approval.
Not my job: Who owns programme scenarios or project controls on that work?
No capacity: Understood. Is there a planning event later when a short conversation would be useful, or should I leave it there?
