# ELI5 and three lines

## Three lines

1. I chose UK electricity transmission and checked public, dated sources on five accounts.
2. For each account, I separate what the sources say from what I'd test, what is unknown and what could block a conversation, and I show where the accounts share the same programmes.
3. I worked one account through to a practice call, without contacting anyone or inventing results.

## Like you're five

Big companies are building the cables and substations that carry electricity around the country. Each job has a plan, and the plan keeps changing.

Planlab makes software that helps people keep those plans honest. A sales development rep's job is to find the people who own those plans and start a useful conversation.

So I made a tidy notebook. It has five pages, one per company. Each page says:
- what the newspapers and official papers actually said, with the link;
- my guess about why that might matter, clearly marked as a guess;
- what I don't know yet, like who the right person is;
- what might stop the conversation.

The notebook also shows that some of these companies work on the same jobs, so five pages don't mean five separate chances.

Then I practised one phone call, carefully, without calling anyone.
